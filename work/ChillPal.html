How generative AI might become a familiar and emotionally accessible interface for care and companionship in later life?

Chill Pal Secretary is a LINE-based conversational system designed to support older adults who experience loneliness, limited social interaction, and difficulty accessing trustworthy information. Grounded in empathy mapping, persona building, and user journey analysis, the project explores how generative AI can function not only as a utility, but also as a form of everyday companionship.

Rather than focusing solely on chat, the system combines emotional support with practical services, including weather and news updates, scam phone verification, health information, fact-checking resources, and AI-generated "elder memes." By choosing LINE—the platform most familiar to our target users—we aimed to lower barriers to adoption and integrate the experience into existing daily routines.

Motivation

In our family group chat, my grandmother often shares news articles, messages, and images, hoping to start a conversation. However, because everyone is busy with their own schedules, her messages often go unanswered. Beyond my own family experience, increasing research and news reports indicate that many elderly people living alone turn to messaging apps to alleviate loneliness. However, this reliance also exposes them to new social risks, such as misinformation and online scams.

Another important cultural context in Taiwan is the phenomenon of "elder memes" (長輩圖)—images with greetings or inspirational messages that older adults frequently share in chat groups. Rather than dismissing this as trivial or outdated, we saw it as a meaningful form of expression and social participation.

Through this project, we ask: How might generative AI become a socially and culturally embedded companion for aging communities?

System Concept

To respond to these insights, we designed Chill Pal Secretary, an AI companion system integrated with LINE that integrates three key features:

  • Generative AI Companion

    • Leveraging generative AI, the system provides continuous conversational feedback, allowing older adults to chat anytime they feel lonely and receive immediate responses.

  • Fact-checking and Reliable Information Access

    • By integrating existing fact-checking platforms and trusted news sources, the system helps users quickly verify information and avoid misinformation.

  • Elder Meme Generator

    • A simple image editor embedded directly within the chat interface enables older adults to easily create and share elder memes. The system can also automatically generate new meme images for users.

Key Features

Welcome Interface

To ensure accessibility for elderly users, the system is integrated directly into LINE, a messaging platform already widely used among older adults in Taiwan.

AI Assistant Chat

Instead of introducing a new application that requires additional learning, the AI assistant operates within a familiar chat interface. When users first add the bot, the system provides a guided onboarding message explaining available services.

Users can then access functions through a large, simplified menu interface, including:

  • Daily greetings
  • Usage Guide
  • Daily News
  • Health information
  • Daily Weather
  • Fact-checking services

This design reduces the learning barrier and allows users to interact with the AI assistant in a way that feels natural and intuitive.

Elder Meme Generator

  1. Users simply tap "Create a New Blessing Image" to enter an intuitive creation space.
  2. By typing a prompt in the top input field and clicking "Generate Image," the system automatically generates a background image.
  3. Users can then add their own blessing text in the lower field, and freely drag, resize, or change the color of the text.
  4. Once finished, they can simply long-press the image to save and share it, allowing older adults to easily create and share their own greeting images.

Information & Safety Support

Users can access useful information through simple text commands or by tapping menu buttons:

  • News – Type "news" or tap Daily News to receive three curated news updates with related links.
  • Weather – Type "[location] + weather" or tap Daily Weather to instantly receive local weather information.
  • Phone Number Verification – Enter a phone number to check its source and identify potential scam calls.
  • Health Information – Tap Health Info to receive articles from trusted health information websites.
  • Fact Checking – Tap Fact Check to open the Taiwan FactCheck Center and easily verify suspicious information.
