Venue Design

// Reflections

"For the first time, I saw how my design could
extend beyond a screen and become part of a collective memory."

For the visual identity, I drew inspiration from the host institution's branding and blended it with local cultural elements from the event location. I also designed and coordinated the production of all official merchandise—this included designing print-ready previews, liaising with vendors, and managing production schedules. In addition, I learned how to manage budgets effectively to deliver high-quality, meaningful gifts within limited financial constraints.

Venue design was the most challenging part. I created various stage backdrops, banners, and directional signage in multiple sizes and formats, ensuring they were stylistically unified yet functionally distinct. This scale of production was unlike anything I had handled before, and only a professional-level event like this could offer such an opportunity.
