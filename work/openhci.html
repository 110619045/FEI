Introduce

OpenHCI is a student-organized workshop on human-computer interaction (HCI), aiming to promote HCI as its main mission. The workshop invites students from engineering, design, and interdisciplinary backgrounds to learn and collaborate across fields, exploring various interaction models within human living spaces. This website serves as the official site for the 12th OpenHCI workshop, providing information such as speaker and organizer profiles, detailed schedules, registration methods, and more.
